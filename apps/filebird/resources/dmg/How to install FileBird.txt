How to install FileBird
==================

FileBird works on any Mac with macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.

1. In this window, drag FileBird onto the Applications folder.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click FileBird.

   The first time, macOS stops it with a message like:
   "Apple could not verify 'FileBird' is free of malware..."
   Click Done. (Don't click Move to Trash.)

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to the Security section. Next to
   "'FileBird' was blocked to protect your Mac", click Open Anyway.
   Confirm with Open Anyway and your password or Touch ID.

4. FileBird opens. You only have to do this once.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older there's a shortcut instead of steps 2-3:
right-click (or Control-click) FileBird in Applications, choose Open, then Open.


Why does macOS warn about FileBird?
------------------------------
FileBird isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate yet, so macOS asks
you to confirm that you trust it before it opens the first time.


If macOS says FileBird "is damaged and can't be opened"
--------------------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and run this line, then open FileBird again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FileBird.app


Uninstalling
------------
Drag FileBird from Applications to the Trash.
